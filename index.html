Notes & Limitations:
• Tributary loads are per pile (user-provided; calculate separately from wind/tributary area using ASCE 7).
• Lateral capacity uses Broms method (free-head pile in cohesionless sand).
• Structural check: combined axial + bending (ASD).
• Timber: Wet service values.
• Concrete: Preliminary ASD (Fc ≈ 0.33 f'c, Fb conservative). For reinforced piles, consult engineer.
• FS = 2.0 on lateral capacity. Add scour/erosion allowance (typically 2–4 ft or 2.5×diameter per FEMA).
• Advanced section: ASCE 7-22 §2.4 ASD combos, §5.4 flood loads, FEMA P-55 scour/debris, NDS 2018 Ch. 3/4/6 timber factors, API RP 2A sand skin friction/end bearing limits. Uplift and compression geotechnical capacities are static estimates without site borings.
• This is a preliminary calculator only. Always consult a licensed structural/geotechnical engineer and site-specific soil borings.
