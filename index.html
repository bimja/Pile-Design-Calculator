Geotechnical

Timber Column Stability (NDS 3.7)

α = share of the fully fixed-head top moment the connection is assumed to provide. 0.5 reflects a semi-rigid bolted timber joint (bolt slip in oversize holes, wood crushing). The moment must also be developed by the connection (checked below); if it can't, fixity can't be counted.
Notched + doubled: M cap = faces × [Fc⊥′·t·(b/3)·(2b/3) bearing couple + bolt couple]; k = 0.5 × faces × (bolt slip γ = 180,000 D1.5 Σr² + Fc⊥ bearing springs); α = kL/(EI + kL); top moment capped at M cap (joint yields in bearing). Only restrains rotation in the plane of the notched beams — the beams must run in the analysis (flood/wind) direction. Net section at the notch checked; FEMA P-55 limits notching to 50% of the pile section.
Sway frame only. With a floor diaphragm tying N pile tops, the struck pile spans fixity → floor as a propped cantilever for the impact; the prop reaction is spread over N piles. Requires the floor framing/diaphragm and beam-to-pile shear connection to deliver the prop reaction (checked separately).
Drift is a serviceability limit with no code value for pile foundations. Service basis: IBC Table 1604.3 note f allows 0.42 × the Vult-level wind for deflection (≈ 0.7 × ASD wind); ASCE 7-22 App. CC suggests a 10-yr MRI wind (≈ 0.3–0.45 × strength on the Texas coast). Strength-level stability (P-delta) stays in the NDS 3.9.2 check.

Flood Loads (ASCE 7-22 Ch. 5 / FEMA P-55)

NDS Table 2.3.2: the impact factor CD = 2.0 is not permitted for members pressure-treated with waterborne preservatives to marine (heavy) retentions, which is typical for coastal timber piles. Use 2.0 only for untreated or other qualifying piles.
Notes & Limitations:
• Tributary loads are per pile. The optional Building Wind Helper fills the per-pile wind share Wh, overturning axial Wot and pile qz (ASCE 7-22 directional); manual entry still works.
• Lateral capacity uses Broms method (free-head pile in cohesionless sand).
• Structural check: combined axial + bending (ASD).
• Timber: Wet service values.
• Concrete: Preliminary ASD (Fc ≈ 0.33 f'c, Fb conservative). For reinforced piles, consult engineer.
• FS = 2.0 on lateral capacity. Add scour/erosion allowance (typically 2–4 ft or 2.5×diameter per FEMA).
• Advanced section: ASCE 7-22 §2.4 ASD combos, §5.4 flood loads, FEMA P-55 scour/debris, NDS 2018 Ch. 3/4/6 timber factors, API RP 2A sand skin friction/end bearing limits. Uplift and compression geotechnical capacities are static estimates without site borings.
• This is a preliminary calculator only. Always consult a licensed structural/geotechnical engineer and site-specific soil borings.
